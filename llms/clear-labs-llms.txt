# Clear Labs

> Clear Labs builds automated next-generation sequencing (NGS) platforms that pair robotics, software, and bioinformatics to deliver genomic data and actionable insights. Its instruments and software serve oncology research (Clear Dx), infectious-disease and microbial genomics (Clear Ix), and food-safety pathogen testing (Clear Safety). Clear Labs publishes no public developer API; this profile is an API Evangelist network company record.
